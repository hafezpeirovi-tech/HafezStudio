Hafez Studio: hybrid editable motion role review routing

Scope
- Fresh Glass review jobs route explicit numbered points to SaaS Title 03.
- Two-word important phrases use SaaS Title 07 on an opaque gradient cutaway.
- Longer important phrases retain the source-bound Editorial portrait compiler.
- Existing chapter templates and shared Editorial palette are retained.
- Native text uses ABAR. Numbered heading/body have separate text/color controls.
- No PNG graphics substitution, automatic final movie export or Upscale coupling.

Evidence and limitations
- Literal ordinal labels may be selected for manual review from source-bound,
  consecutive ASR words with confidence >= 0.5. They are never promoted to trusted
  text and never generate a fabricated explanatory body.
- All other chapter/caption trust checks remain unchanged.
- MOTION_REVIEW_FA.txt lists selected cue times and uncertain labels.
- Native gallery checked title/emphasis/numbered roles; a separate full-length
  hybrid review saved 15 MOGRT layers, including one current-media portrait.
- Native read-only inspection confirmed 15 instances. Numbered and portrait
  appearance checked inside Premiere. These checks do not certify a final video.
- Generic six-second intro remains. Charts/tables/curated music are not included.
- Vendor internal Poppins-SemiBold dependency still prompts on this machine;
  exposed ABAR text rendered correctly. Do not silently install substitute fonts.

Release
- Engine only: same installed app shell, Reels and Upscale remain unchanged.
- Exact-job compiled portrait evidence must never be relocated or rewritten to
  satisfy a smoke test. Validate it at its original root; validate vendor-only
  role routing separately against installed resource identities.
- Build script accepts a date/revision and verifies source hashes before/after.
- Activation validates packaged runtime, stages and hashes all engine files,
  refuses active app/jobs, preserves the previous engine and rolls back failures.
- HAFEZ_GLASS_MOTION_ROLES_QA=0 explicitly restores legacy routing.

Tests: 86 Glass tests, 13 Editorial tests and Glass finisher contract test passed.
